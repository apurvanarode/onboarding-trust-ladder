MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Acme Systems, Inc.
Document Reference: LEGAL-NDA-001
Effective Date: Upon Countersignature

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme Systems, Inc.
("Company") and the undersigned party ("Recipient") to protect confidential information shared
in the course of a professional relationship.

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
   Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to, proprietary source code, software
   architecture, algorithms, customer data, personally identifiable information (PII), business
   strategies, financial projections, and any other non-public materials disclosed by either party.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECIPIENT
   The Recipient agrees to: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not
   disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent; (c) use Confidential
   Information solely for the purposes agreed upon in writing.

3. EXCLUSIONS
   This Agreement does not apply to information that is: (a) publicly available through no fault
   of the Recipient; (b) independently developed without use of Confidential Information; (c)
   disclosed pursuant to a lawful court order, provided the disclosing party is given prior notice.

4. TERM
   This Agreement remains in effect for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date, unless
   terminated earlier by mutual written agreement.

5. REMEDIES
   Any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm. Acme Systems reserves the right to
   seek injunctive relief in addition to all other available legal remedies.

6. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which Acme Systems, Inc.
   is incorporated.
