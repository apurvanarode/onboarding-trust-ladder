CUSTOMER DATA HANDLING POLICY

Acme Systems, Inc.
Document Reference: POLICY-DATA-002
Version: 2.1 | Status: Active

This policy establishes mandatory rules governing the use of customer data across all engineering,
quality assurance, and data science activities at Acme Systems, Inc.

1. SCOPE
   This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who have
   access to Acme Systems production systems or customer data in any form.

2. PROHIBITION ON PRODUCTION DATA IN NON-PRODUCTION ENVIRONMENTS
   Under no circumstances shall real production customer data — including but not limited to
   personally identifiable information (PII), payment records, usage logs, or account credentials —
   be copied, exported, or used within development, staging, or testing environments.

3. REQUIREMENT FOR SYNTHETIC DATA
   All development and testing activities that require representative data sets must use
   synthetically generated data only. Synthetic data must be created using approved internal
   tooling or a vendor tool listed on the Acme Systems Approved Tools Register.

4. ACCESS CONTROLS
   Access to production data is restricted on a need-to-know basis and must be approved by a
   designated Data Owner. All access events are logged and subject to periodic audit.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING
   Any accidental exposure of production customer data to a non-production environment must be
   reported to the Information Security team within two (2) hours of discovery via the internal
   incident management system.

6. ENFORCEMENT
   Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination
   of employment or contract, and may be referred to appropriate regulatory authorities where
   required by applicable law.
